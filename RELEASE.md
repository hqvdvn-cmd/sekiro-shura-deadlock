# Shura v1.0

Wolf/Shura replaces Yamato, with swords, attack and ability effects, a dark ultimate appearance and Shura selection UI.

- Keeps reload A, selected after an in-game A/B comparison.
- Adds matching painted normal, low-health and killstreak portraits. The killstreak portrait has black hair and a glowing red eye.
- Includes the selection-hand, back-sword and first-ability aim corrections from the preceding builds.

The voice pack remains unfinished. Some synthetic Japanese lines sound choppy; voice replacement is the next planned update. A brief blue parry startup flash may remain.

Download `sekiro-shura-v1.0-standalone-r2.zip`. With Dota 2 → Deadlock, also use `sekiro-shura-v1.0-dota-patch-r2.zip` and disable the standalone ultimate-binding component. Full manual and manager instructions are in the EN/RU README.

All package hashes, CRCs, portrait pixels and selected native resources were checked. The owner selected A in game; the latest low-health/killstreak portraits have texture checks. No separate clean standalone match is claimed.
